# stet — pricing

Cloud bills the project, never the people — reviewers are signed links, not seats. The self-hosted edition is complete and free. Paid tiers are waitlisted until Cloud launch.

## Self-hosted — $0

For developers running their own infrastructure. The whole mechanism — not a demo.

Forever · Apache-2.0 · single-user. Includes:
- Package, CLI, MCP server, dashboards
- Environments & promotion · packs
- SEO layer with offline checks
- Your Postgres — or no database at all (upgrade to a store any time)

## Solo — $19/mo

For one person shipping one product.

1 project · paid monthly. Everything in Self-hosted, and:
- Hosted store & sign-in
- Publish scheduling
- 5 GB media · 1k hosted contacts (meters never hard-stop)
- Docs + community support

## Team — $39/mo

For teams where more than one person touches the words.

3 projects · extra projects $10/mo. Everything in Solo, and:
- Owner · developer · editor roles
- Review & approvals (simple mode for non-technical editors)
- Client review links — $0 per reviewer
- 25 GB media · 10k hosted contacts
- Email support

## Business — $149/mo

For companies that need retention, audit and priority answers.

10 projects · extra projects $10/mo. Everything in Team, and:
- Audit retention, 24 months
- 100 GB media · 50k hosted contacts
- Priority support
- SSO/SAML attaches here (roadmap — priced at build)

## Add-ons and meters

- **Extra project** ($10/mo) — Each additional project on a Team or Business plan. Same mechanism, same review links, no new seats to buy.
- **Managed sending** (waitlist) — stet sends broadcasts from your own domain — no ESP account needed. The price is set at first activation; activation takes weeks, not minutes.
- **Media** (5 / 25 / 100 GB) — Included by tier; overage is metered. Meters bill, they never hard-stop your site.
- **Hosted contacts** (1k / 10k / 50k) — Included by tier; overage metered. Self-hosted contacts live in your own Postgres and are never billed.
- **Reviewers** ($0) — Always. A reviewer is a signed link, not an account — client sign-off never costs a seat.
- **SSO / SAML** (roadmap) — Always a priced add-on; the price is set at build. Attaches to Business.
